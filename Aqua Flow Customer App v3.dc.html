What changed vs customer v2
Photography instead of placeholders
Every hero, shop, product and rider image is a real photo in assets/photos/, sized and cropped for its slot — no empty drop targets left in the flow.
Gradient headers, light body
Home, shop detail and My jars open on a teal→blue gradient that rounds off into an #F1F7FC body, so the eye lands on the search field and the products.
Cards, not dividers
Shops, products, orders and notifications are 20px cards on a soft shadow instead of hairline-separated rows.
Icon-tile tab bar
The active tab is a filled gradient tile — readable at a glance, and the same shape language as the supplier v2 app.
Deposit and OTP get real weight
The delivery OTP is a full gradient card with 4 large digits; the jar-return stepper and its deposit warning sit in their own tinted block in the cart.
Same rules, unchanged
Minimum order, Rs 40 delivery, Rs 500 / Rs 350 deposits, 2-of-3 jar exchange, 60-second acceptance window, 5-attempt OTP lock, 5 km service radius — all identical to v2.
